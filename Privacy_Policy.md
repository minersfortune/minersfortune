Miner's Fortune & Chain Privacy Policy

I. Welcome to the Miner's Tavern

Welcome, miner, merchant, and adventurer, to the world of Miner's Fortune & Chain.

The moment you pick up your pickaxe and set foot on this land, you become part of this medieval world. Like a tavern keeper, we remember your name, your story, every gem you've dug up, and every choice you've made.

This notice (or "Privacy Policy") tells you how we treat your information during your adventure—with the same care and respect we give to your minecart and your gold coins.

II. What We Remember

To give you a complete adventure experience, we keep the following "adventure logs" about you:

Your Identity Badge: When you sign in with Apple ID, we record your user ID and nickname—like the adventurer nameplate hanging on the tavern wall, so we can recognize you and keep your progress from getting mixed up with others.

Your Miner's Backpack: Your game progress, level records, achievement badges, and the gold, gems, and goods you've worked hard to mine. These are your possessions—we're just keeping them safe so they're still there when you log in next time.

Your Travel Footprints: How far you've ventured into the underground mines, which fork in the tunnel you chose, what you said to which NPC at the tavern—we keep these operation logs because it's these choices that make up your unique story. (Rest assured, we don't eavesdrop on your private chats with NPCs.)

Your Gear Information: Your device model, operating system version, and IP address—just like a blacksmith needs to know whether you use a one-handed sword or a two-handed axe, we need to know your device info to make sure the game runs smoothly for you.

Your Trading Ledger: If you buy gems or items at the market, we keep your transaction records. This ensures your coins don't vanish into thin air and that we can help with after-sales service when needed.

The Tavern's Operation Logs: Game launch times, feature clicks, session duration, and the occasional crash log—just like a tavern keeper tracks how many guests come each night and which ale is most popular, so the tavern can be run better.

III. What We Use This Information For

We keep these records not to pry into your privacy, but:

So you can continue your adventure: Account login and progress sync—you can pick up right where you left off in the mine.

To make the tavern better: By analyzing how everyone plays, we adjust level difficulty, fine-tune trade prices, and write better story branches—so you always find something new each time you visit.

To keep goblins at bay: Preventing cheating, fraud, and malicious disruption—maintaining fairness and order in this land.

To give you a shout now and then: When necessary (like game updates, important notices, or when your goods have been sold), we'll reach out via in-game notifications or email.

IV. We Don't Sell Your Information to Anyone

We make this solemn promise:

We do not and will not sell your personal information to anyone. Not once. Not ever.

We don't make money by selling your data—we make money when you buy gems and items in the game, and that's what keeps us making games for you.

We only share your information with necessary partners in these cases:

Working with Craft Masters: We collaborate with a handful of analytics service providers and payment processors (like the one who helps complete your purchase). They work only under our authorization and must protect your information just as we do.

The King's Law: If laws and regulations require it, or if government authorities make a lawful request, we may have to disclose your information—just like when the king's herald arrives, even the tavern keeper can't say no.

V. How We Protect Your Treasure

We use industry-standard encryption (SSL/TLS and the like) to protect your data—like using iron doors and chains to guard the treasures deep in the mine. We keep your information only as long as necessary for the purposes described above—except when the law requires us to keep records like transaction history for a longer period, and we comply with that.

VI. Your Rights

You have the right to review, correct, or delete the information we have about you in the tavern at any time. You may also withdraw your consent at any time. Just contact us via the methods at the end of this notice, and we'll treat your request with the seriousness it deserves—like we would for any regular patron.

VII. Young Adventurers

Our tavern does not currently serve adventurers under 13 years old (or the lower age specified by your region). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you discover that a young one has wandered into our world, please let us know and we will clear the relevant records immediately.

VIII. Notice Updates

This notice may be revised from time to time. If there are significant changes, we'll post a notification on the game login screen or on our official website. We suggest you glance at it once in a while.
